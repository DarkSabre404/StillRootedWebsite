Our Operating Model

The Still Rooted engagement model maps individual elder backgrounds directly onto localized institutional needs. We run a three-stage integration framework to onboard senior participants smoothly:

1. Profile Asset Mapping: Gathering structural data regarding past technical disciplines, languages spoken, and structural trades directly from our field surveys.


2. Curated Pairing: Aligning volunteer availability targets with structural deficits identified in neighborhood schools and community activity centers.


3. Monitored Implementation: Executing physical interaction models (workshops, direct pairings) backed by structured feedback loops to optimize senior safety and child learning speeds.
