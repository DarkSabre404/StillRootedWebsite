Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience or qualifications to volunteer or mentor?

No prior experience or formal qualifications are required. We welcome people who are willing to share their time, practical skills, and life experience.

Is there any fee to take part?

There is no fee to take part in our community activities. If an event has any specific costs, the team will share those details in advance.

How do I hear about upcoming workshops and events?

Check the Events & Calendar page for upcoming workshops and community gatherings. New events appear there as they are scheduled.
